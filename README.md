## What I'm building

Claiming Social Security is the largest irreversible financial decision most Americans make — and 96% of households get it wrong, leaving roughly $111,000 on the table each (United Income, 2019).

MySSAgent optimizes the decision itself:

- **A deterministic SSA-compliant engine** enumerates every monthly claiming age from 62 to 70 against three longevity horizons — 291 scenario combinations for an individual, ~296 for a couple, plus 7 interacting rule checks (spousal, survivor, ex-spouse, earnings test, restricted application, WEP, GPO). The math never runs inside an LLM.
- **Maxine**, the guidance layer, runs on the Claude API — she explains the strategy in plain language and answers the follow-ups.
- **A credentialed RSSA®** reviews the complex cases. A real human trust layer, not a disclaimer.
- **No SSN ever enters the platform.** Client-side parsing; data minimization by design.

Sold direct to consumers, and to independent RIAs and insurance professionals as credential-additive infrastructure — it works alongside their advice rather than replacing it.

The same engine spine extends to the rest of the federal benefits stack: Medicare, IRMAA, SSDI, VA.
